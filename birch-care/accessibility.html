Accessibility Statement

Birch Care Group is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including parents, young people, social workers and colleagues who may be using assistive technology, have low vision, or find complex layouts difficult to navigate.

What We've Built In

  • Strong colour contrast: body text uses our dark forest green on ivory or white (well above the WCAG AA 4.5:1 minimum), and our accent gold is used only for borders, highlights and small details — never as body text on a light background — because it does not meet contrast requirements on its own.
  • Readable typography: a clear serif/sans-serif pairing, generous line-height, and text that scales with the browser's font-size settings rather than being fixed in place.
  • Straightforward navigation: a consistent header and footer on every page, a visible "Skip to main content" link for keyboard users, and a current page indicator in the main menu.
  • Keyboard accessibility: every link, button and form field can be reached and operated by keyboard alone, with a clearly visible focus outline.
  • Descriptive alt text: images include meaningful alternative text for screen reader users (decorative images are hidden from assistive technology).
  • Reduced motion support: animations and smooth-scrolling are disabled automatically for visitors who have requested reduced motion in their operating system settings.

Target Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA standard throughout this site.

Ongoing work: this is a first build using temporary stock imagery and placeholder content. Before launch, all replacement photography should include proper alt text, and the site should be tested with a real screen reader (e.g. NVDA or VoiceOver) and a full keyboard-only pass, not just automated tools.

Reporting an Accessibility Problem

If you find a part of this website that's difficult to use, please let us know at accessibility@birchcaregroup.co.uk (placeholder address) and we'll do our best to fix it.
