Small Homes, Big Hearts

We believe every child deserves a stable, loving home environment. Our homes are deliberately small in size so that every young person is known, heard and cared for as an individual — not a number.

Why Choose Birch Care Group

A Family Feel, With Professional Support

Safety & Stability

Secure, well-maintained homes with consistent routines and a dedicated, trained staff team, giving every child a reliable foundation to build on.

Nurturing Relationships

Our trauma-informed approach puts relationships first, helping young people build trust, self-worth and healthy connections with the adults around them.

Growth & Independence

From education support to life-skills coaching, we help every young person develop the confidence and skills they need to thrive into adulthood.
