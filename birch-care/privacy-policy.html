Who We Are

Birch Care Group ("we", "us", "our") provides residential care homes for children and young people in North East England. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you use this website or interact with us.

What Information We Collect

We may collect: contact details you provide through our enquiry forms (name, email, phone number, message content); referral and placement information provided by local authorities and social workers; and limited technical information about how this website is used.

We do not knowingly collect special category data (including data about children in our care) through this public website. Referral and case information about children is handled separately, through secure channels, under our data protection and safeguarding procedures — not through this site.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, process placement referrals, manage recruitment applications, and meet our legal and regulatory obligations (including to Ofsted and local authorities).

Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of: your consent (e.g. submitting an enquiry form); our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and operating this website; and compliance with our legal obligations as a regulated children's home provider.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep enquiry and contact information only as long as necessary to respond to you and meet our legal obligations. Records relating to children in our care are retained in line with statutory retention requirements for children's social care records, which are considerably longer and governed by separate policies.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with local authorities, regulators (such as Ofsted), and professional partners (such as education or health providers) where necessary for a child's care, or where we are legally required to.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies

This website does not currently set non-essential cookies. If analytics, marketing or chat tools are added in future, this policy and an accompanying cookie banner will need to be updated accordingly.

Contact Us

For any questions about this policy or your data, contact privacy@birchcaregroup.co.uk (placeholder address). You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Birch Care Group provides nurturing, Ofsted-registered residential care homes for children and young people across North East England.
