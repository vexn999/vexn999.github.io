About Birch Care Group

Support, nurture and thrive aren't just words on our logo — they're the standard every member of our team is held to, every day.

Our Story

Birch Care Group was founded to give children and young people across North East England somewhere they could truly call home while in residential care — a small, warm, family-style setting rather than an institutional one. Every home we run is built around the same idea: that children thrive when they feel safe, known and believed in.

Our Mission

To provide safe, nurturing residential homes where children and young people are supported to heal, grow in confidence, and build the skills and relationships they need to thrive into independent adulthood.

Our Values

Support

We meet every young person where they are, with patient, consistent, person-centred support from a team that genuinely cares.

Nurture

We create calm, warm, homely environments where relationships come before rules, and every child feels valued as an individual.

Thrive

We focus on the future as much as the present — education, life skills and confidence-building that set young people up to succeed.

Our Approach

We take a trauma-informed, relationship-first approach to residential care. Our staff are trained to understand the impact of adverse childhood experiences and to respond with patience, consistency and compassion rather than punitive discipline. Every young person has an individual care plan built around their own needs, strengths and goals, developed together with social workers, education providers and, wherever possible, their own family.
