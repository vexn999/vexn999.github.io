Our Services

Care that goes far beyond a roof over their head — here's how we support every young person in our care.

Residential Care

24-hour, person-centred residential care in a safe, homely setting, delivered by a consistent, trained staff team.

Education & Learning Support

We work closely with schools, virtual schools and tutors to keep every young person's education on track, including support for those out of mainstream school.

Therapeutic & Emotional Support

A trauma-informed approach, with access to therapeutic input, helps young people understand and process their experiences in a safe, supported way.

Health & Wellbeing

Support accessing GP, dental, mental health and specialist health services, plus encouragement towards healthy routines, hobbies and physical activity.

Independent Living Preparation

Practical life-skills coaching — budgeting, cooking, job applications and more — to prepare older teens for the transition to independent living.

Family Time & Contact Support

Where it's safe and appropriate, we support supervised family contact and work with social workers to maintain positive family relationships.
